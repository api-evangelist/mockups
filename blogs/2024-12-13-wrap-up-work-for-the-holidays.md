The weeks before the holidays can often be a fallow time for companies. Scheduling can be challenging as employees juggle both personal and professional commitments. And it’s hard to launch new initiatives just before a break. So what can you do to stay productive as the end of year approaches? Well, this pause in momentum is a perfect opportunity for wrap-up, reflection, reviews, and planning. We’ve put together some templates and suggestions to help your team make the most of the next few weeks. It’s also worth figuring out what work will need to be carried forward into the new year.
